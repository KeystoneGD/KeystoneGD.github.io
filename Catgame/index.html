How to play

  1. Put exactly one cat in every row and every column.
  2. Each coloured patch gets exactly one cat.
  3. Cats won't nap touching each other, not even at the corners.

Tap a square once to leave a paw mark (no cat here), twice for a cat, three times to clear it. On a PC, right-click to drop a cat straight in. Drag across squares to paw-mark lots at once. A cat in the wrong spot costs a heart; lose all three and the level starts over. On a keyboard, use the arrows, Space, X, C and Ctrl+Z. Every puzzle has one solution and can be solved by logic alone.
