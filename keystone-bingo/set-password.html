Fit the lock

Runs entirely in this browser

Choose the username and password for the caller’s box. Neither one leaves this page: what comes out the other end is a salt and a short encrypted token, which is what you commit to the repository. Nobody reading your source can work backwards to the password without guessing it.

Pick something long. Four or five unrelated words beats one clever word with symbols in it.
This is a client-side lock, not a server. A long password is what makes it hold.

Commit it, push, and sign in at host.html. Changing your mind later is the same three steps. If you lose the password there is nothing to recover — generate a new lock instead.
