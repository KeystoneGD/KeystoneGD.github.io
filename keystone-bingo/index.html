House rules

  1. One sheet per player, six tickets to a sheet. Every ticket carries fifteen numbers.
  2. Dab your own numbers. Nothing marks itself — miss one and it stays missed.
  3. Shout BINGO the moment you’re on. A claim only stands while the winning number is still up: once the next ball drops, that prize has gone.
  4. Your claim goes straight to the host, who rebuilds your ticket from it and checks it against the numbers actually called.
  5. A false claim costs nothing the first time. Keep doing it and you’re out of the room.
  6. Two callers at once share the prize, at the host’s discretion.
  7. Quiet during the calls, please. The host’s decision is final.

The stages

Numbers keep coming through the whole game. First a line — any five across on one ticket. Then two lines on that same ticket. Then the full house: all fifteen.
