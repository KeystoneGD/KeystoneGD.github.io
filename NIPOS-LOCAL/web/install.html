Put NIPOS on your phone

NIPOS opens like an app from its own icon, full screen, with alerts. It's free, and it updates itself, so there's nothing to download or reinstall.

You're already using NIPOS from your Home Screen. Open NIPOS

iPhone and iPad

  1. Open the NIPOS start screen in Safari. Do the next steps from that page, not this one. Open the NIPOS start screen
  2. Tap the Share button. On an iPhone it's at the bottom of the screen (on newer iPhones, tap ••• first); on an iPad it's at the top right.
  3. Scroll down the list and tap Add to Home Screen.
    Add to Home Screen
  4. If you see Open as Web App, leave it switched on. Tap Add.
    Open as Web App
  5. Open NIPOS from your Home Screen and sign in with your PIN. You'll see only the parts of NIPOS your account can use. To get alerts, open the Till or Orders and tap Turn on alerts.

Alerts on iPhone need iOS 16.4 or newer (Settings › General › Software Update).

Android

  1. Open the NIPOS start screen in Chrome. Open the NIPOS start screen
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu at the top right, then Add to Home screen (or Install app), then Install.
  3. Open NIPOS from your Home screen and sign in with your PIN.
